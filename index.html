The idea

Democracy should be something we do every day.

People experience public decisions every day. Daily Referendum aims to make participation easier, public priorities visible and government follow-through easier to track.

01

Give everyone a voice

Make it easier for people to raise issues, answer public questions and contribute practical proposals.

02

Make public opinion visible

Present public questions, participation results and supporting context clearly and transparently.

03

Hold power accountable

Track issues, official responses, commitments and progress — including when matters remain unresolved.

A public voice matters when it is heard, recorded and followed by action.

The Daily Referendum approach

From public questions to visible accountability.

The platform is being developed around six connected activities: daily questions, public issues, petitions and proposals, structured debate, government accountability and public insight.

1

Ask and participate

People take part in clearly worded public questions and share their views on issues that affect the country.

2

Raise issues and propose solutions

Citizens submit problems, evidence, petitions and practical ideas for public consideration.

3

Track accountability

Follow submissions through responses, commitments, progress updates and independently reported outcomes.

Public issue spotlight · Cape Town

Housing pressure is visible. What should decision-makers prioritise?

A recent City of Cape Town report points to a sharp increase in mapped informal structures. This is a complex human and planning challenge — one that deserves evidence, lived experience and practical solutions, not blame.

Reported change · 2016–2025

Informal structures increased by about 67% in nine years.

The City's latest Spatial Trends and Implementation Tracking Report records an increase from 169,330 mapped structures in 2016 to 283,379 in 2025. The mapped area of informality also grew from roughly 1,200 to more than 3,100 hectares.

These are counts of structures, not people or households. The City says the figure excludes areas of unlawful land occupation, so it should not be treated as a complete count of all informal structures.

What should the public ask decision-makers to prioritise?

These are public discussion prompts, not a live vote. Daily Referendum is being developed; voting and issue submissions are not yet active.

01Expand access to affordable, well-located housing and understand barriers to delivery.
02Improve water, sanitation, electricity, refuse removal and safe access in existing settlements.
03Identify suitable land and explain constraints such as flood risk, wetlands, transport and services.
04Publish clear budgets, targets, timelines and progress for housing and settlement upgrades.

Sources: City of Cape Town: Spatial Trends Report (9 Nov 2026) · The South African: report summary. This issue spotlight is intended to prompt informed discussion; it does not imply endorsement by the City or affected communities.

Build participation. Earn trust.

Start in South Africa. Grow with the people.

The first release will focus on South African issues and participation. We will test the experience, establish transparent rules and build confidence before expanding the platform nationally.

Proposed first phase

One country. Many voices. Shared accountability.

The platform will be developed with input from citizens and civic organisations. Its voting methodology, privacy safeguards and moderation rules will be published before public participation opens.

⌖
A measured roadmap

Trust is built through transparency.

Daily Referendum will be developed in stages, with clear participation rules, privacy safeguards, published voting methodology and visible progress. The stages below are a roadmap, not a claim that these features are already live.

Phase 01

Listen & define

Agree the pilot location, priorities, community input and participation rules.

Phase 02

Build & test

Create a simple, accessible platform and test it with a small group of residents.

Phase 03

Publish & report

Share submissions in an appropriate, privacy-conscious way and report progress clearly.

Phase 04

Learn & improve

Evaluate what worked, publish lessons and decide whether to expand.
