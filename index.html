The idea

Public decisions should never feel out of reach.

People experience the results of public decisions every day — in their streets, services, schools and communities. Yet it can be difficult to know where to raise an issue, how to contribute, or whether anything changed.

01

Make participation practical

Give residents clearer ways to raise local priorities, contribute ideas and take part in constructive discussion.

02

Make information accessible

Help people understand the issues, the decisions being considered and the responsibilities of public institutions.

03

Make follow-through visible

Encourage clear updates on what was heard, what action is proposed and what progress has — or has not — been made.

A voice matters most when people can see that it was heard — and what happened because of it.

A principle for how this initiative should work.

How it could work

From community input to public accountability.

The concept is deliberately straightforward. Start with a defined local area, listen to the issues residents identify, and create a transparent record of priorities and progress.

1

Listen locally

Residents identify the issues that matter most in their neighbourhood and share practical suggestions.

2

Organise the priorities

Group submissions into clear themes so common concerns and different perspectives can be understood.

3

Track the response

Publish updates that distinguish between issues raised, responses received, actions underway and unresolved matters.

Start small. Build trust.

A local pilot before a bigger platform.

Rather than promise a national solution from day one, the initiative can be tested in one city or community. The first objective is to learn what residents actually need and prove that the process is useful, open and manageable.

Proposed first phase

One community. Clear priorities. Visible progress.

The pilot area, community partners and participation process still need to be agreed. The platform should be shaped with residents, not designed on their behalf.

⌖
A measured roadmap

Build it in the open.

A credible civic platform needs clear rules, privacy safeguards and a transparent process. These are the proposed stages — not claims that the work is already complete.

Phase 01

Listen & define

Agree the pilot location, priorities, community input and participation rules.

Phase 02

Build & test

Create a simple, accessible platform and test it with a small group of residents.

Phase 03

Publish & report

Share submissions in an appropriate, privacy-conscious way and report progress clearly.

Phase 04

Learn & improve

Evaluate what worked, publish lessons and decide whether to expand.

The next step

Help shape a government that listens.

This is an early-stage initiative. The next step is to bring together people who want to help define the pilot, its safeguards and the way it will serve the public.
